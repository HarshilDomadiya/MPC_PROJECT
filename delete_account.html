PlantIQ Account Deletion

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Request Account Deletion

PlantIQ users can request deletion of their PlantIQ account and associated data by contacting us at:

harshildomadiya5@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your PlantIQ account and include "PlantIQ Account Deletion Request" in the subject line.

What Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, we will take reasonable steps to delete data associated with the user's PlantIQ account, including:

  • PlantIQ user account information
  • User profile information
  • Plants associated with the account
  • Connected device association information
  • Plant sensor history associated with the account
  • Plant and device settings associated with the account

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required or permitted by applicable law, for security purposes, to prevent fraud or abuse, or for legitimate operational purposes.

Processing of Deletion Requests

After receiving a valid deletion request, we will take reasonable steps to process the request and delete the applicable account information.

If additional information is reasonably necessary to verify the request, we may contact the requester using the provided email address.
