PlantIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

PlantIQ ("PlantIQ", "we", "us", or "our") is an IoT-based plant monitoring application that allows users to monitor plant conditions and, where supported by the connected hardware, control plant watering.

This Privacy Policy explains what information PlantIQ collects, how that information is used, how it is stored, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create a PlantIQ account, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Firebase Authentication account identifier
  • Account creation information

1.2 Plant and Device Information

When you add or connect a plant and IoT device, PlantIQ may store:

  • Plant name
  • Plant identifier
  • Connected device identifier
  • Device connection status
  • Device last-seen information
  • Device claiming information required to connect the device

1.3 Sensor and Plant Monitoring Data

PlantIQ may receive and store data generated by the connected ESP32-based device, including:

  • Soil moisture readings
  • Temperature readings
  • Humidity readings
  • Sensor connection status
  • Water pump status
  • Reading timestamps

1.4 Settings

PlantIQ may store plant and device settings selected by the user, such as automatic watering settings and moisture thresholds.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to provide and operate PlantIQ, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Authenticating users
  • Connecting users with their registered plants and devices
  • Displaying plant sensor readings
  • Displaying historical sensor information
  • Showing device connection status
  • Managing automatic watering settings
  • Controlling and displaying watering status where supported
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of the service
  • Responding to user support and privacy requests

3. Firebase Services

PlantIQ uses Firebase services provided by Google to support authentication and cloud data storage.

PlantIQ uses Firebase Authentication to manage user accounts and Firebase Realtime Database to store information associated with users, plants, connected devices, sensor readings, settings, and related application data.

Firebase data is protected using authentication and database access rules intended to restrict access to authorized users and devices.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate PlantIQ, including Google Firebase services used for authentication and cloud database functionality.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal processes, or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with PlantIQ accounts and devices. Access to user-specific information is controlled using Firebase Authentication and database security rules.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide PlantIQ functionality, maintain the service, comply with applicable obligations, and resolve disputes or support requests.

Users may request deletion of their PlantIQ account and associated personal information using the account deletion functionality provided by the application or by contacting us using the email address below.
